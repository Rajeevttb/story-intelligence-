Market Gaps = Your Opportunity CRITICAL GAPS

No Affordable CBSE

Gap between FREE govt schools and ₹20K+/year CBSE. A school at ₹12-18K/year with CBSE + English would dominate the rural aspirational segment (20% of market).

No Village Transport Network

73.6% of population is rural. No school aggressively markets bus/van routes to villages. Transport to 15-20 villages = massive catchment + competitive moat.

No Spoken English Program

"English bolna sikhayenge" is the #1 parent aspiration. No school highlights a dedicated spoken English program. Low cost to implement, very high parent appeal.

No ICSE / National Chain

Zero ICSE schools. Zero national brands (DPS, DAV, Ryan, Kidzee). First mover advantage is wide open for a branded entry.

No Digital Marketing

No school has a parent app, active social media, or Google My Business optimization. Daily WhatsApp updates alone = significant differentiation.

Narkhed Also Underserved

Adjacent taluka has NO prominent CBSE school. A strong school in Katol could pull students from both talukas — doubling the addressable market.
