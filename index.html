1. Fix the Hook — First 1 Second Must Stop the Scroll

Start with a provocative statement, not a title card. Use close-up faces with raw emotion as the first frame. Text overlay with a contradiction ("He had everything... except the one thing that mattered"). Never start with music or a logo.

2. Adopt the "DM Test" for Every Story

Before posting, ask: "Would someone screenshot this and send it to a friend?" If not, the emotional core isn't sharp enough. Stories should trigger a "YOU NEED TO SEE THIS" reflex.

3. Use the 3-Word Caption Strategy

Move away from long captions. Use 3-word captions that create intrigue: "Father. Grain. Tears." / "One. Good. Teacher." / "Mother. Front. Row." — Put the full story in the first comment.

4. Post Timing & Frequency Optimization

Post Reels between 7-9 PM IST. Minimum 5 Reels/week with at least 2 "micro-stories" (under 30 seconds). Use carousel posts for "untold story" series. Stories daily with polls: "Should we tell this story?"

5. Build a "Story Radar" Community Loop

End every Reel with: "Send us stories nobody is telling" with a DM prompt. Feature user-submitted stories monthly. Create an "Unseen Stories" highlight reel. Turns followers into scouts.

Content Directions GROWTH

A. "The Story Behind the Viral" Series

Take stories that went viral for surface reasons and tell the DEEPER human angle nobody covered. Positions Tribal Box as the page that goes ONE LAYER DEEPER.

B. "60-Second Human Portraits"

Create a signature format: 60-second, one-person, one-emotion stories. No B-roll montages. Just a face, a voice, and a truth. Use stripped-down piano audio. Creates a distinctive visual identity.

C. "3 AM Stories" — Things People Think But Never Say
