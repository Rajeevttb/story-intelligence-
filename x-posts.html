Trending: #EndRacism #March21 #Sharpeville
On this day in 1960, in Sharpeville, South Africa: 5,000 Black South Africans gathered outside a police station. Their crime? Refusing to carry "pass books" — documents that dictated where they could live, work, and travel. At 1:30 PM, police fired 1,344 rounds. No warning. 69 killed. 180 wounded. Many shot in the back while running. The UN declared March 21 the day the world confronts racial discrimination. 66 years later, billions still carry documents that define where they belong and where they do not. The weapon changed. The system didn't. #EndRacism #Sharpeville #IDERD
