Trending: Avantibai Lodhi / First Freedom Struggle
On this day in 1858, Rani Avantibai Lodhi pierced herself with her own sword. She chose death over capture by the British. Before that, she raised an army of 4,000. Defeated Deputy Commissioner Waddington. Forced the British to flee. When neighbouring kings refused to fight, she sent them bangles with a message: "If you have duty towards our motherland, raise your swords. Otherwise wear these and hide." 168 years later, most Indians don't know her name. She deserves more than a trending hashtag. #AvantibaiLodhi #FreedomFighters
